# AI Usage Statement for the Activity Template Library

This statement describes AI assistance in designing and expanding activity template content. Team members jointly designed the template format. AI supported feasibility checks and discussions, while the team made the final decisions on the structure, fields and presentation.

## AI Assistance

The team established two seed templates, Colour Hunt and Follow the Leader, as references for content organisation, age differences and step structure. Based on these seeds, AI assisted with expanding the remaining 16 activity templates, suggesting activity steps, age adaptations and parent guidance.

AI-assisted content was treated as draft material and revised and reviewed by people before inclusion in the library. AI did not decide which content to accept and did not act as a human reviewer.

## Human Involvement and Responsibility

Team members jointly designed and confirmed the template format. Jiabin coordinated content organisation, requirements and revisions. As confirmed by Jiabin, lychen completed the content review of all 18 migrated templates, which are recorded as `reviewed`.

The team remains responsible for accepting, modifying and using AI-assisted content and for the final project deliverables.

## Validation Results and Limitations

The current suite of 45 automated tests passed. These tests cover template structure, loading and export, separation of draft and reviewed content, photo verification fields, safety-rule rejection cases and selected environmental conditions.

Automated checks primarily validate structure and explicit rules. They do not establish that activities are safe, age-appropriate or enjoyable in every setting, and they do not replace practical testing.

The 20/40/60-minute labels follow the team's adopted 3/5/7-step rule; they are not measured durations. Photo verification hooks are available, but all current template steps use manual tap confirmation. Photo-based confirmation will only be enabled for suitable steps after the relevant visual targets have been measured and approved for use.

This statement does not claim completed field testing with children, measured photo verification accuracy or full deployment validation. Any such claims must be supported by evidence actually collected by the team.
